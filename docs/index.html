NetCap Planner

Forecast bandwidth demand, see when a link runs out of headroom, and get the date to order more capacity. Runs entirely in your browser.

Inputs

CSV format

One row per day. Either date,gbps (e.g. 2026-01-15,42.3) or just one column of daily peak Gbps. At least 28 days.

How the forecast works
  • The model is Holt-Winters triple exponential smoothing with a 7-day season, which captures level, trend and the weekday/weekend cycle.
  • Smoothing parameters are chosen by grid search, minimising one-step-ahead error on your history.
  • Accuracy (MAPE) comes from a backtest: the model is fitted without the last 14 days, then forecasts them.
  • The 90% range widens with the forecast distance, based on the model's own residuals.
  • Growth scenarios compound an extra monthly increase on top of the baseline forecast.
  • Recommended capacity is the next standard link size that keeps the forecast peak at or below 70% utilisation.
Forecasts are statistical estimates. Validate against business plans before placing orders.
